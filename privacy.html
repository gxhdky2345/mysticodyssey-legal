Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Effective date: November 21, 2026 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 21 日

GameCampfire Studio (“we”, “us”) provides the mobile game Mystic Odyssey: Idle RPG (“the Game”). This policy explains what information the Game processes and why. 本政策说明《玄域征途》（Mystic Odyssey: Idle RPG，下称“本游戏”）会处理哪些信息、为什么处理，以及你有哪些权利。

1. Information processed · 我们处理的信息

1.1 Game progress · 游戏存档

Character name, level, equipment, virtual items and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the Game or clearing app data deletes this data. This version has no account system and no cloud save.
角色名、等级、装备、虚拟物品和设置保存在你的设备本地。卸载游戏或清除应用数据会删除这些数据。本版本没有账号系统，也没有云存档。

1.2 Network time · 网络时间校准

After you consent to this policy, the Game may request the current time from public websites (www.baidu.com, www.apple.com) to calculate offline rewards correctly. The request does not include your account or save data. Those websites can see the IP address required to deliver the response.
在你同意本政策后，游戏可能向公共网站（www.baidu.com、www.apple.com）请求当前时间，用于正确结算离线收益。请求中不附带账号或存档。对方处理请求时可以获得网络通信所需的 IP 地址。

1.3 Advertising · 广告服务

Rewarded videos and app-open ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). The advertising SDK is initialized only after you consent to this policy. Where required, iOS also asks for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission; declining affects ad personalization only and does not limit gameplay.
激励视频和开屏广告由 Google AdMob（Google LLC）提供。只有在你同意本政策后才会初始化广告 SDK。在需要时，iOS 还会请求“允许跟踪”（ATT）授权；拒绝只影响个性化广告，不影响游戏功能。

For advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • Device or other identifiers, including the advertising identifier and app set ID;
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • Device model, operating system and app version;
  • Ad impressions, clicks and related interaction data.

广告投放、分析与防欺诈过程中，Google Mobile Ads SDK 可能收集并共享：设备或其他标识符（含广告标识符、App Set ID）、IP 地址及由此推断的粗略位置、设备型号与系统版本、广告展示与点击等交互记录。详见 Google 隐私政策。

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings. 你可以在系统设置中重置或关闭广告标识符。

1.4 What we do not collect · 我们不收集的信息

The Game does not require registration, and does not collect your name, phone number, ID number, contacts, photos or precise location. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location permissions.
本游戏无需注册，不收集你的姓名、手机号、身份证号、通讯录、照片或精确位置，也不申请相机、麦克风、通讯录或定位权限。

2. Device permissions · 设备权限

  • Network (INTERNET) — network time, ad loading. 网络：取时与广告加载。
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — detect connectivity before network calls. 网络状态：请求前判断网络。
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, Android) — ad delivery and measurement; can be reset in system settings. 广告 ID：广告投放与效果衡量，可在系统设置重置。

3. Children · 未成年人

The Game is suitable for users aged 12 and over. Users under 14 should use it with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian.
本游戏适合 12 周岁及以上用户。未满 14 周岁的未成年人请在监护人同意和指导下使用。

4. Retention and security · 存储与安全

Save data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Game. We use reasonable technical measures to protect local data, but no method can guarantee absolute security. Ad data handled by Google is retained according to Google’s policies.
存档保存在你的设备上，直到你删除或卸载游戏。我们采取合理的技术措施保护本地数据，但任何技术措施都无法保证绝对安全。Google 处理的广告数据按其政策保留。

5. Your rights · 你的权利

  • Delete local data in-game via System → Delete Save, or uninstall the Game. 游戏内【系统】→“删除存档”，或卸载游戏。
  • Reset or limit ad personalization in system settings. 在系统设置中重置或限制广告个性化。
  • Contact us at campfiregame@agentmail.to. 通过邮箱联系我们。

6. Changes · 政策更新

If this policy changes materially, we will ask you to review it again at launch. 本政策发生重大变更时，我们会在游戏启动时再次请你阅读并确认。
