Frequently asked questions · 常见问题

Where is my progress saved? · 存档保存在哪里？

Progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing app data deletes it. There is no account and no cloud save in this version.
游戏进度保存在本机。卸载游戏或清除应用数据会删除存档。本版本没有账号系统，也没有云存档。

How do I earn diamonds and gold? · 钻石和金币怎么获得？

Idle rewards accumulate offline, and optional rewarded videos grant extra diamonds, gold and viewing tokens. Ads are always optional.
离线会累积挂机收益；自愿观看激励视频可获得额外钻石、金币和观影令。广告完全自愿。

Can I remove ads? · 可以去掉广告吗？

This version is free and supported by ads; there is no paid ad-removal option and no in-app purchase.
本版本免费，通过广告支持开发；没有付费去广告，也没有应用内购买。

How do I reset my save? · 如何重置存档？

Open the game → System → Delete Save. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data.
游戏内【系统】→“删除存档”；卸载游戏同样会删除全部本地数据。
